# Entry 1: Choosing a Topic (Medical Records)
##### 10/19/26

## Why Medical Records?

For a while, I had a bleak outlook on my future. I thought there would be no sustainable job I would enjoy. However, I'm not sure why, but I started thinking about working in cybersecurity, and asked my parents about it. My mom told me that healthcare institutions require skilled cybersecurity workers to protect medical records, as **they contain patients' personal information** and, as such, **are often targeted by hackers**. A job in that field sounded appealing to me, so I figured I could use this project as an opportunity to research how these records used to and currently work, how technology has been implemented in their storage and protection, and how the system could be improved upon in the future.
